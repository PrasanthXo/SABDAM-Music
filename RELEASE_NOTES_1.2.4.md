# SABDHAM 1.2.4

## Home top bar
- Restores a richer dark-green background with soft glow and depth.
- Slightly increases the SABDHAM logo size.
- Keeps the compact layout and avoids the old busy waveform design.

## Update delivery
- This release is optional, not forced.
- The app does not display the raw APK download URL.
- Future optional updates are routed away from the blocking update dialog and into SABDHAM's notification-bell experience.

- versionName: 1.2.4
